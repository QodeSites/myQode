# Reply to Razorpay — HDFC business category clarification

Dear Team,

Thank you for flagging this. We can confirm the exact nature of our services.

## Nature of services

Qode Advisors LLP is a **SEBI-registered Portfolio Manager**, registration
number **INP000008914**. We provide **Portfolio Management Services (PMS)** —
we manage investment portfolios on behalf of our clients under a signed PMS
agreement.

We do **not** provide investment advisory services. We are not registered as an
Investment Adviser and do not offer fee-for-advice, research, tips or
recommendations to the public.

A point of clarification that may explain the observation: **"Advisors" appears
in our registered legal name (Qode Advisors LLP), not as a description of our
services.** Our SEBI registration number carries the **INP** prefix, which SEBI
issues exclusively to Portfolio Managers. Investment Advisers are registered
under a separate **INA** prefix, which does not apply to us.

## What payments on the website are for

Payments collected through our platform are **client contributions into their
own managed investment portfolios** — capital the client is investing, not
consideration for advice. Our revenue is earned through management fees and
performance fees charged against those portfolios under the PMS agreement,
in line with SEBI's PMS Regulations, 2020.

Client funds are held with a SEBI-registered custodian, and payments are
accepted only via Net Banking and UPI from the investor's own registered bank
account.
